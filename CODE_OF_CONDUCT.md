# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone in the security research and bug bounty community.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Supporting ethical security research practices
- Helping newcomers to bug bounty and security research

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Sharing exploits for unauthorized use
- Encouraging illegal hacking activity
- Publishing others' private information
- Misusing this toolkit against unauthorized targets

## Security Research Ethics

Contributors and users are expected to:
- Only test systems they own or have explicit written permission to test
- Follow responsible disclosure when finding real vulnerabilities
- Respect privacy and data protection laws

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to **ktaki928@gmail.com**.
